# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Creators and marketers managing their social media posts.

## Product Purpose
A complete end-user social media management platform. Success looks like users effectively and efficiently managing their content.

## Positioning
An AI-driven platform for automating social media posts.

## Brand Personality
Confident, Minimal, Efficient. The interface should evoke reliability and speed.

## Anti-references
Avoid cluttered, noisy SaaS dashboards with too many cards and borders.

## Design Principles
- **Focus on content**: Keep the layout straightforward and content-centric (inspired by tools like Buffer or Hootsuite).
- **Efficiency over decoration**: Minimize visual noise and reduce the use of unnecessary cards or borders.
- **Expert confidence**: The AI-driven features should feel reliable and understated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No specific accessibility requirements at this time.
